Questions?
We've got answers.

Currently Machine Learning, SQL & Databases, Statistics, and Python — with questions across easy, medium, and hard difficulties. More questions and topics are added regularly.

DatalystLM uses the Web Speech API built into your browser — no third-party transcription service, no audio sent to external servers. Chrome gives the best results. Your voice stays on your device.

Feedback is powered by Claude (Anthropic). You can also run DatalystLM with a local Ollama model if you prefer full offline privacy.

Reading activates recognition memory. Saying an answer out loud activates recall — the same process you use in an actual interview. Active recall produces better retention and reduces anxiety under pressure.

Your transcribed answers are sent to Claude for evaluation but are not stored beyond the session. Practice sessions and scores are saved to your account for progress tracking.
